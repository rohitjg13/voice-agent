# TalentFlow Objection FAQ

## "We already use LinkedIn Recruiter / SeekOut / Gem"

Most TalentFlow customers had one of those tools before switching or augmenting. The typical comparison: LinkedIn Recruiter is great for raw search, but recruiters spend hours filtering and personalizing outreach. SeekOut is strong on diversity data, but the outreach automation is limited. Gem has solid sequencing, but limited sourcing.

TalentFlow combines AI-driven sourcing (across LinkedIn, GitHub, and proprietary sources) with personalized outreach generation. A Series C fintech we work with cut sourcer time per role from 8 hours to 90 minutes while doubling reply rates.

## "It's too expensive"

TalentFlow pricing scales with team size:
- 1-5 recruiters: $1,500/month
- 6-15 recruiters: $3,500/month
- 16+ recruiters: custom pricing

ROI math: a typical recruiter spends ~40% of their week on sourcing (~16 hours). TalentFlow reduces that to ~5 hours, freeing ~11 hours/week per recruiter. At a fully-loaded recruiter cost of $90K/year, that's ~$40K/year of recovered capacity per recruiter.

For a team of 5 recruiters, that's $200K/year of recovered productivity against an $18K/year subscription. Most teams break even in month 1.

## "Our internal team handles recruiting"

That's exactly who TalentFlow is built for. We only sell to in-house teams — we don't compete with agencies or staffing firms. Our customers report their recruiters are 3-4x more productive on sourcing, which is why they don't need to bring in agencies for senior roles anymore.

A common pattern: companies that were spending $50-80K per senior hire on agency fees cut that to zero within 6 months of adopting TalentFlow.

## "We don't have time to implement a new tool"

Implementation takes about 90 minutes total. Our team handles all the heavy lifting:
- ATS integration: 30 minutes (Greenhouse, Workday, Lever, Ashby — native one-click setup)
- Role specs and ICP setup: 30 minutes
- Recruiter onboarding: 30 minutes

Most teams are sourcing their first candidates within 2 hours of signing.

## "How is this different from ChatGPT for recruiting?"

ChatGPT can draft outreach messages, but it can't find candidates. TalentFlow's sourcing engine searches across LinkedIn, GitHub, professional networks, and proprietary sources — surfacing passive candidates ChatGPT has never seen.

We also enforce candidate-side guardrails (frequency caps, opt-out tracking, anti-spam) that a general-purpose LLM can't.

## "We tried AI sourcing before — it didn't work"

Most early "AI sourcing" tools were keyword matching with extra steps. TalentFlow uses a fine-tuned model that understands job requirements semantically — it can find an "API platform engineer" even if the candidate's LinkedIn just says "backend developer at Stripe."

Our average customer sees 3-5x better fit scores compared to keyword-based tools.

Totally understand. Most decisions involve the Head of Talent, the VP of People, and sometimes Finance. We can send a custom ROI summary tailored to your hiring volume that you can share internally — usually leads to a faster yes.

The fastest decision we've seen was 4 days; the longest was 6 weeks. Either way, no pressure on this call.
