# TalentFlow Product FAQ

## What does TalentFlow do?

TalentFlow is an AI-powered sourcing and outreach platform for in-house recruiting teams. It surfaces passive candidates that match a role spec, generates personalized outreach, and syncs everything back to your ATS.

The three core capabilities are: (1) AI sourcing across LinkedIn, GitHub, and proprietary sources, (2) automated multi-channel personalized outreach, and (3) ATS sync with Greenhouse, Workday, Lever, and Ashby.

## What ATS systems do you integrate with?

Native integrations with Greenhouse, Workday, Lever, Ashby, BambooHR, and JazzHR. Integration setup takes about 30 minutes via a one-click OAuth flow.

For other systems, we offer a REST API and pre-built Zapier connectors. About 95% of our customers use one of the native integrations.

## How does the AI sourcing work?

TalentFlow uses a fine-tuned semantic search model trained on millions of job-to-candidate matches. You input a role spec (or paste a job description), and the engine returns ranked candidate profiles with fit scores explaining why each candidate matched.

Unlike keyword search, the model understands that "backend platform engineer" might match someone whose title is "senior software engineer" at a company with a known platform team.

## How does the outreach automation work?

Once you've selected candidates, TalentFlow generates personalized first-touch messages and follow-up sequences. The messages reference specific candidate signals — recent projects, talks they've given, open-source contributions — not generic templates.

Recruiters review and approve messages before they send (or enable auto-send mode for high-confidence sequences). The platform tracks replies, opt-outs, and sequence performance.

Typical results: 25-35% reply rates vs. 8-12% for templated outreach.

## Who are typical TalentFlow customers?

In-house recruiting teams at venture-backed startups (Series A through pre-IPO) and growth-stage public companies. Most customers have 3-25 recruiters and are hiring 50-500 roles per year.

We don't sell to staffing firms, recruitment agencies, or RPOs. We only support direct-hire in-house teams.

## Is candidate data secure?

TalentFlow is SOC 2 Type II audited. All candidate data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We sign DPAs with every customer, support GDPR data subject access requests, and offer EU data residency for European customers.

Candidate data is never used to train models or shared with other customers.
