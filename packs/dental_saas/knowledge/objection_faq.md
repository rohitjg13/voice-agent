# DentaFlow Objection FAQ

## "We're happy with our current software"

Most offices that tell us this are using Dentrix, Eaglesoft, or Open Dental. DentaFlow isn't a replacement — it layers on top of what you already have, adding automated patient reminders and insurance verification without disrupting existing workflows. The average switchover time for offices that did fully migrate was 6 hours.

A dental group in Chicago told us the exact same thing before they tried DentaFlow. Within 90 days, their no-show rate dropped from 18% to 11%. They kept their existing EHR and just added our reminder layer.

DentaFlow pricing starts at $299/month for practices with up to 3 operatories. A single recovered no-show appointment generates $150–$400 in revenue depending on the procedure. Most practices recover the full monthly subscription in under 3 appointments per month.

We offer a 30-day free trial — no credit card required. During the trial, the average practice prevents 8–12 no-shows, which covers the first year of the subscription in revenue recovered.

## "We don't have time to implement new software"

Implementation is handled entirely by our onboarding team. The typical setup takes 4–6 hours of staff time spread across 2 days. We integrate with your existing patient database — no manual data entry required. Most offices are live within 48 hours of signing.

We schedule the onboarding at a time that works for the practice, typically on a slower afternoon. The front desk team needs about 90 minutes of training.

## "We're not the decision maker"

The most common decision-making structure in dental practices is: office manager handles day-to-day software decisions, Dr. (owner) approves any spend over $500/month. DentaFlow can send a personalized ROI summary directly to the practice owner — many have approved it via email without needing an additional meeting.

## "We already send reminders"

Manual reminder calls and basic text reminders have an average response rate of 40–50%. DentaFlow's multi-channel automated system (text + email + voice call) achieves 78% confirmation rates. The difference is the timing logic — DentaFlow sends reminders at the optimal intervals (72 hours, 24 hours, 2 hours before appointment) and escalates channels when a patient doesn't respond.

The system also handles patient responses automatically — rescheduling, cancellation, and confirmation without any staff involvement.
