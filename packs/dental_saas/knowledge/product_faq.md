# DentaFlow Product FAQ

## What does DentaFlow do?

DentaFlow is a cloud-based platform that helps dental practices reduce no-shows, automate insurance verification, and manage patient communications from a single dashboard. It integrates with all major dental EHR/practice management systems.

## How does the no-show reduction work?

DentaFlow sends automated reminders via text message, email, and voice call at 72 hours, 24 hours, and 2 hours before each appointment. Patients can confirm, cancel, or request a reschedule directly from the reminder — no phone call to the front desk required. When a cancellation comes in, the system automatically fills the slot from a waitlist.

Practices using DentaFlow report an average 38% reduction in no-shows within the first 30 days.

## What practice management systems does DentaFlow integrate with?

DentaFlow has native integrations with: Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Carestream, Curve Dental, and Dolphin. For other systems, we provide a universal CSV import and API access.

Integration is bidirectional — appointment changes in DentaFlow sync back to your existing system automatically.

## How long does setup take?

Setup typically takes 4–6 hours of staff time over 2 days. Our onboarding team handles all technical configuration. The front desk staff training session is 90 minutes and includes hands-on practice with common scenarios.

Most practices go live within 48 hours of signing.

## Is DentaFlow secure?

DentaFlow is SOC 2 Type II certified. All patient data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with every practice, which is required for HIPAA compliance. Patient data is never sold or shared with third parties.

## How does insurance verification work?

DentaFlow automatically verifies insurance eligibility for every scheduled patient 48 hours before their appointment. Results appear in the dashboard — co-pay amounts, deductible status, and coverage details — without any staff action. For failed verifications, the system flags the patient and suggests next steps.

Manual verifications that used to take 20 minutes now happen automatically in 10–30 seconds.
